# Datapad

> Datapad is an autonomous AI data analyst platform that lets business teams analyze their data by asking questions in natural language. It connects to 50+ data sources (SQL, BigQuery, Snowflake, Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Shopify, HubSpot and more) and uses Text2SQL and Python code generation to turn questions into queries, dashboards, and insights, with a Slack bot for conversational analytics. Datapad is a San Francisco company backed by a16z. It is an end-user SaaS product and does not currently publish a public developer REST/GraphQL API, SDKs, or webhook surface.
